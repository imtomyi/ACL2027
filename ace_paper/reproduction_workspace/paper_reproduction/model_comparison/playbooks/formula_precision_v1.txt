## OUTPUT FORMAT
[fmt-00001] Return `final_answer` as a plain base-10 number only. Do not include a percent sign, currency symbol, unit label, explanatory text, or thousands separators.
[fmt-00002] Use a decimal point when needed, but do not add unnecessary precision. Round to the nearest hundredth only when the exact result has more than two decimal places.
[fmt-00003] Keep all intermediate calculations at full precision and round only the final result.

## RATIO AND PERCENTAGE CONVENTIONS
[calc-00004] When the requested metric is conventionally expressed as a percentage, such as margin, rate, yield, return, or percentage change, compute the ratio and multiply it by 100 before returning `final_answer` unless the question explicitly requests a decimal ratio.
[calc-00005] Distinguish a decimal ratio from percentage points: a ratio of 0.15 corresponds to a percentage-form answer of 15.0, not 0.15.
[calc-00006] For Operating Margin, use `(Operating Income / Revenue) * 100`. For example, Operating Income 45000 and Revenue 300000 produce 15.0.
[calc-00007] For percentage change, use `((new_value - old_value) / old_value) * 100`. Preserve the sign of decreases.

## UNIT AND SCALE CHECKS
[unit-00008] Normalize magnitude words before calculating: thousand means 1000, million means 1000000, and billion means 1000000000.
[unit-00009] Convert all operands to compatible units before applying a formula. Do not cancel or preserve a magnitude word implicitly.
