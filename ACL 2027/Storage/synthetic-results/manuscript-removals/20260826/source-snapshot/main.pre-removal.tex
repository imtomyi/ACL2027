\begin{abstract}
Large language models can assist qualitative coding, but specialist review does not scale easily. We present \textbf{WarrantRoute}, a source-grounded protocol for testing which reviewer expertise is needed for particular qualitative judgments and for allocating review under a fixed expert-time budget. The planned study will compare researchers without specialist expertise, qualitative methods experts, and domain experts on natural outputs and controlled variants targeting unsupported evidence, source concentration, counterevidence loss, contextual flattening, and unsupported abstraction. It will evaluate whether a policy frozen before held-out testing can route items without hiding less frequent or negative cases, and whether selected feedback repairs defects without introducing new ones. The current manuscript reports the protocol and a measurement-pipeline qualification on entirely fictional packets; it reports no real-corpus model, rater, routing, or repair result.
\end{abstract}
