## Draft request

**Project title:** WarrantRoute: evidence routing and qualitative-analysis auditing for ACL 2027

**Request:** Please determine the institutional review, information-security, data-protection, contractual, and human-subjects requirements for secondary analysis of four dialogue/text corpora and for the proposed model-assisted and human-reviewed evaluation. Please state the exact approved scope, conditions, protocol/determination version, effective date, re-review or expiry date, and approving authority.

**Draft purpose (PI verification required):** WarrantRoute is a noncommercial academic study of evidence routing and qualitative-analysis auditing. It is not intended to train models, identify or contact participants, redistribute source data, or support commercial use.

## Data scope to verify

- **Dreaddit:** minimum restricted text and lineage needed for the approved study. Treat use as blocked pending Reddit confirmation through the current research route; do not assume a historical archive or prior collection is exempt from current platform requirements.
- **AGYW/community-men focus groups:** minimum transcript text and focus-group/local-speaker lineage. The public deposit/license is evidence to review, not a project-specific institutional determination.
- **KODIS:** only complete human-human dialogues, with conversation/speaker/turn lineage; exclude surveys, demographics, and human-AI dialogue.
- **CANDOR:** request only `No Video Media (~10GB)` through BetterUp, then retain only original per-conversation Cliffhanger transcript CSV fields if present. Exclude audio, video, surveys, demographics, precise timestamps, and raw source identifiers. The exact authorized package and field contract remain unverified until receipt.

Across all corpora, pseudonymous IDs will replace working identifiers; source-linked speakers/conversations/groups/posts will remain together across experimental splits. Pseudonymization is not anonymity and the project will not call the data anonymous.

## Proposed controls to evaluate

- Restricted storage and least-privilege access; no source text or identity crosswalk in tracked repository governance files.
- Exact version/hash receipt records and frozen corpus-specific input contracts before use.
- Model processing only through separately approved endpoint profiles specifying provider, tenant, product/endpoint, region, exact model/snapshot/API, terms/privacy versions, training-use status, retention/deletion, human access, subprocessors, and expiry.
- Two-person contextual privacy review for every excerpt shown to a model/rater or considered for quotation/release; reviewers recorded only as `REV_…` pseudonyms in the project log.
- Rater access limited by corpus and role, with current privacy training, confidentiality acknowledgment, consent/withdrawal process, encryption, and access logging.
- Cluster-aware sampling and splitting so linked source units cannot cross experimental splits.
- Provider- and institution-specific retention/deletion deadlines, documented deletion verification, incident response, and named accountability in the approved external system.
- Release limited to expressly approved artifact classes. Model-processing or rater-display clearance will not be treated as quotation/release clearance.

## Decisions requested from the institution

Please expressly address, separately for each corpus and version:

1. Whether the proposed secondary use is approved, exempt, not-human-subjects research, or requires another pathway.
2. Whether sensitive/protected text may be sent to each proposed model endpoint under the exact technical and contractual configuration.
3. Whether and under what consent/confidentiality terms human raters may view excerpts and surrounding context.
4. Whether the proposed pseudonymization, two-person contextual privacy review, and cluster handling are sufficient, and what additional safeguards are required.
5. Whether quotations or other outputs may be published and what excerpt, attribution, deletion-aware-publication, and re-review rules apply.
6. Required retention periods, deletion deadlines, backup handling, incident response, audit logs, data locations, and cross-border/subprocessor constraints.
7. Whether each individual source-data user/rater needs separate approval, training, agreement, or registration.
8. What changes trigger amendment or re-review, including source/provider terms, model endpoint/snapshot, rater service, corpus version, or release scope.
