## Why execution is stopped

The current local governance record reports both requested corpora at `0/10`
completed real-text gates and `real_text_ready=false`. The restricted source and
working copies are present, but possession and structural preparation are not
authorization. No real record is approved for model processing, human-rater
display, quotation, or experiment use.

Required gates are:

1. project-specific institutional determination;
2. exact source/platform authorization;
3. approved model/provider processing profile;
4. exact corpus/version/input-field contract;
5. two-person privacy review of every selected excerpt and displayed context;
6. cluster-aware sampling and a power/time-derived count;
7. a frozen study manifest, prospectively before held-out access;
8. rater consent, withdrawal, least privilege, encryption, and access logs;
9. retention, deletion, incident-response, and responsible-owner controls; and
10. release-class, license/attribution, and release privacy controls.

Dreaddit additionally lacks a dataset license and archived source ethics/privacy
statement, so institutional and Reddit/platform confirmation must expressly
cover the planned uses. AGYW's CC BY 4.0 deposit and upstream consent/ethics
statements do not replace this project's secondary-use, model-processing,
rater-exposure, contextual-privacy, or release determination.
