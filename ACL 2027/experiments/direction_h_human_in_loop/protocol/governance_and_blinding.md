# Direction H governance and blinding plan

Governance version: `direction-h-governance-v1`  
Date: 2026-08-25 (America/Chicago)  
Current authorization: synthetic qualification only.

### Selected-lane amendment

The selected companion-compatible phase is the exact 24-item Direction J
browser lane. Its reviewer display and response area contain only fictional
items, the shared guide, exact actor-neutral ratings, eligible timing receipts,
and post-rating identity-free feedback. A non-Charlie activation record selects
that queue and retires the overlapping four-item full-output queue before any
item display. References below to a “live four-item pilot,” `DHQ-*`, or
`qc-paper-metrics-v1` describe the retained internal rehearsal only.

For the selected phase Charlie's study-scoped Direction J actor ID is `charlie`;
his Direction H alias and construction-involved researcher role remain in
separate coordinator records. The browser hides candidate identity, condition,
and other ratings, but the shared developer workspace does not establish secure
technical separation. Report the v1 run as condition-masked developer review.
The private Direction J item key may be opened only after all 24 rating/timing/
feedback triplets are locked, and only by the authorized non-Charlie
coordinator/verifier.

## 1. Non-negotiable boundary

Direction H may use fictional synthetic packets now. It is **not cleared to
process protected real text**.

No team member may open protected source-derived text for Direction H, derive a
real-text item, send real text to a generator or revision model, deploy it in a
rating instrument, or expose it to Charlie or another rater until every gate in
Section 4 is affirmatively documented. A public download, an original study's
ethics approval, a corpus paper's consent statement, or an existing local copy is
not by itself authorization for this secondary study, model processing, or rater
exposure.

The controlling shared sources are:

- the real-data stop conditions in
  [`qc-analytic-contract-v1`](../../qualitative_coding_baselines/protocol/analytic_contract.md);
- the corpus-specific approval plan in
  [`overleaf/DATASET_PLAN.md`](../../../overleaf/DATASET_PLAN.md); and
- the manuscript ethics boundary in
  [`overleaf/sections/paper.tex`](../../../overleaf/sections/paper.tex).

When documents disagree, apply the more protective restriction and record the
conflict for institutional review. Do not resolve permission uncertainty by
assuming that research usefulness outweighs it.

## 2. Data classes and current status

| Data class | Direction H status | Permitted use now |
|---|---|---|
| Fictional packets and fictional controlled variants | Eligible after schema, lineage, and label checks | Instrument qualification, Charlie pilot, model revision qualification, and synthetic adjudication |
| Synthetic qualification model outputs | Eligible through blinded derived copies; historical files remain immutable | Item construction and workflow qualification without showing historical judge scores or model identities |
| Dreaddit source-derived text | Not cleared | None for Direction H until all gates pass |
| CaCHe source-derived text | Not cleared | None for Direction H until all gates pass |
| KODIS or CANDOR source-derived text | Not cleared; authorized real distributions are not established in the shared baseline | None |
| Human rating, rationale, timing, or positionality data | New research data; study determination and access controls apply | Synthetic pilot only under the documented local/approved procedure |
| Private blind maps and planted labels | Restricted study-control data | Custodian and authorized analyst access only; never reviewer/model visible |

Synthetic results cannot establish validity on any real corpus and must never be
pooled with real-corpus outcomes.

The retained internal reviewer-safe manifest has four fictional items. The coordinator map
records three intended single-defect manipulations and one
`no_planted_defect_control`. Those labels are construction intentions, not
independent validation and not reviewer-visible assignments. Agent or automatic
QA establishes workflow integrity only. A non-Charlie construction verifier must
confirm the intended manipulation/control status before any record enters a
detection, false-positive, or specificity analysis. The no-planted-defect label
does not assert that an output is universally free of natural defects.

## 3. Charlie's role and independence boundary

Charlie serves as the Direction H developer-researcher case reviewer. Because he
helped develop the study, the following separations are mandatory:

- his canonical rating says `evaluator_group = researcher` for measurement
  compatibility;
- the separate rater registry records
  `case_classification = developer_researcher_case_baseline`,
  `helped_develop_study = true`, relevant `prior_access`,
  `blinding_status = condition_masked_developer` for the v1 shared-workspace
  pilot; a future `condition_blinded_developer` classification requires a new
  frozen instrument and an independent prospective access-control attestation,
  and
  `include_in_primary_independent_human_pool = false`;
- `helped_construct_assigned_items` remains a separate item-specific fact and is
  recorded as false only when the coordinator has confirmed Charlie did not
  construct the four assigned pilot items;
- he does not verify the controlled label or construct the specific item he will
  rate;
- he does not hold or inspect the private model/condition/label map before his
  ratings and feedback are locked;
- he does not see companion LLM ratings, historical model-judge outputs, or other
  human responses before submission;
- he does not operate a discretionary repair of his own rating after unblinding;
- he does not adjudicate revisions produced from his feedback; and
- he is not counted as an independent reviewer, reference-panel member, or
  universal human standard.

"Independent" may describe that Charlie submits each rating without consulting
another rater. It may not describe his relationship to study development. If a
report cannot state this distinction clearly, use `masked developer-researcher
case review` rather than `blinded independent human review`.

## 4. Gates required before any protected real text

Every gate must have an owner, dated evidence, scope, expiration or review date,
and final `cleared` decision. Unknown, pending, inferred, or partially applicable
means **not cleared**.

### Gate G1: institutional determination

- The institution has reviewed this specific secondary use.
- The determination explicitly covers source-text preparation, evidence-packet
  display, a new human-rater study, model generation, model revision, feedback
  processing, timing/rationale collection, withdrawal, and planned release.
- The responsible protocol or determination number, date, and approved team roles
  are recorded.

### Gate G2: source, license, and platform authorization

- The exact corpus version and source terms are recorded.
- Planned excerpt display, transformation, model processing, and release are each
  allowed.
- Dreaddit has a documented institutional and applicable Reddit/platform basis;
  the absence of a dataset license or author-consent procedure is not ignored.
- CaCHe secondary use, CC BY attribution, model processing, and rater exposure are
  confirmed for this project despite the public deposit.
- KODIS or CANDOR cannot enter the study until the authorized distribution and its
  access agreement are available and checked.

### Gate G3: model/provider processing approval

- The exact generator, companion-rater, and revision-model providers and snapshots
  are named.
- Processing location, retention, training use, logging, subcontractors, abuse
  monitoring, deletion, and cross-border terms are approved for the corpus.
- The approval covers prompts, excerpts, generated outputs, human feedback, and
  rationales, not source text alone.
- Only approved endpoints and accounts are configured. A model that is acceptable
  for public synthetic data is not automatically acceptable for protected text.

### Gate G4: exact data version, fields, and lineage

- Received-file checksums and the approved analysis-copy manifest are frozen.
- Allowed and prohibited fields are explicit.
- Project identifiers replace source identifiers where required while a protected
  linkage map is held separately.
- Context windows and source clusters are preserved without importing unnecessary
  dates, locations, labels, engagement metadata, or other identifiers.
- The packet-to-source lineage is reproducible and access logged.

### Gate G5: privacy and excerpt clearance

- Every excerpt shown to a rater, sent to a model, or considered for release has a
  documented two-person privacy and permissions review.
- Direct identifiers and unnecessary indirect identifiers are removed or masked.
- Sensitive short-text hashes are evaluated for linkage risk rather than released
  automatically.
- Exact quotations are used only when the approved task requires them. A
  paraphrase is labeled as such and is never used in quotation-integrity trials.
- The approved excerpt set, context limits, and residual risks are frozen.

### Gate G6: sampling and leakage controls

- Sampling preserves Dreaddit post clusters and CaCHe focus-group and
  transcript-local speaker clusters.
- No several-turn or several-segment set is treated as independent people.
- Dreaddit train remains development; Dreaddit test remains the secondary
  in-domain audit; all CaCHe groups remain held-out cross-domain confirmation
  unless a formally approved manuscript change is made.
- AGYW/CaCHe text, outputs, or published themes have not influenced prompts,
  controlled variants, thresholds, budgets, outcomes, or stopping rules.
- The model, prompt, rating task, feature set, item construction, exclusion rules,
  revision formatter, adjudication rule, and manifest are frozen before held-out
  access.

### Gate G7: rater study protections

- Consent covers evidence exposure, potentially sensitive content, ratings,
  rationales, timing, role/positionality fields, withdrawal, retention, and
  release.
- Participation is voluntary and separate from employment, grading, supervision,
  performance evaluation, and authorship decisions.
- Eligibility, role definition, compensation or workload credit, training,
  comprehension checks, accessibility, withdrawal, and adverse-event procedures
  are approved and frozen.
- Identifiers and withdrawal tokens are stored separately from response data.
- Free-text rationales are privacy-screened before sharing or release.

### Gate G8: security, retention, and incident response

- Named storage locations, access roles, encryption, backup, retention periods,
  deletion procedures, and access-log review are documented.
- Participant-facing deployment does not ship protected text in a public client
  bundle or expose organizer views through participant credentials.
- Participant and organizer secrets are distinct and appropriately rotated.
- A breach, accidental unblinding, unauthorized model call, or text disclosure
  has a stop, containment, notification, and review procedure.

### Gate G9: frozen run and study manifests

- The item manifest, blind-map hash, rater assignment manifest, task/schema
  versions, revision freeze manifest, randomization seed custody, and analysis
  version validate.
- The confirmatory access log begins before the held-out material is opened.
- No result-bearing file exists in a template or example form that could be
  mistaken for observation.

## 5. Role separation and least privilege

One person may hold more than one project role only where the study status allows
it and the overlap is disclosed. For any item contributing to an inferential
endpoint, incompatible roles remain separate.

| Role | May access | Must not access before lock |
|---|---|---|
| Item constructor | Source/synthetic packet, construction guide | Assigned reviewer response or revision outcome |
| Manipulation verifier | Clean/defect pair and target label | Assigned reviewer's future rating |
| Blind-map custodian | Private model, run, base, and condition maps | May not rate or adjudicate mapped items |
| Reviewer (Charlie or independent human) | Reviewer-visible blind packet and own current form | Model identity, planted label, twin, other ratings, outcome revisions |
| Feedback formatter/operator | Locked rating and frozen formatter | Private expected answer beyond required machine linkage |
| Revision operator | Frozen cases, feedback payloads, runtime manifest | Human-readable condition interpretations or outcome judgments |
| Repair adjudicator | Blind revision packet and adjudication instructions | Feedback source/condition, model identity, other adjudications |
| Analyst | Locked, validated analysis tables | Held-out mappings before code and contrasts are frozen |

If staffing cannot provide a separate custodian, an automated deterministic
packager may perform the mapping. The private map must be encrypted, access
logged, or held outside the reviewer's readable workspace until submission. If
Charlie can inspect the private map because of his developer access and no
effective procedural or technical segregation exists, the study must report the
items as **masked, not blinded**. Self-restraint alone is a limitation, not a
technical blind.

## 6. Blinding objects

The study maintains distinct mappings with separate access:

1. source record to project source identifier;
2. base packet to clean/controlled variants;
3. `packet_id`/`output_id` to generator, run, and model identity;
4. blind item to target defect and target flag;
5. rating to feedback record;
6. revision output to `feedback_arm` and repeat; and
7. adjudication blind ID to revision output.

Reviewer-facing files contain only the minimum links needed to read the packet
and submit a response. The canonical rating record contains no generator,
condition, manipulation, expected-answer, or other-rater field. File names,
directory names, ordering, text headers, JSON keys, timestamps, and style must
also be checked for label leakage.

Private maps are hashed before use. Hashes may appear in the run manifest; the
map contents do not appear in a public or reviewer bundle.

## 7. Blinding timeline

### Phase B0: construction and freeze

- Constructors create or derive synthetic no-planted-defect and single-defect
  items.
- For new items, a non-Charlie verifier fixes the target family/control status
  and matching canonical flag before assignment. The live four-item pilot has
  only coordinator-committed intended labels at present; its verifier must remain
  blind to Charlie's responses and may confirm the intended label or mark the
  item ineligible in a `direction-h-construction-verification-v1` record, but may
  not retarget it after rating.
- Eligibility is itemwise: only a committed controlled-defect item with
  disposition `verified_for_detection` proceeds to paired revision. Overall or
  control-item status does not veto a different eligible target. Every
  `workflow_only` and rejected item remains in private exclusion accounting.
- A non-Charlie coordinator completes the minimal arm-blind target-assessment
  briefs for exactly those eligible targets and commits the exact file hash
  before revision cases are materialized or any model call occurs.
- The custodian assigns blind IDs, checks presentation parity, separates twins,
  records hashes, and freezes the manifest.
- Tutorial examples and pilot/confirmatory items are disjoint.

### Phase B1: Charlie rating

Charlie sees only the analytic brief, evidence packet, blind output, and canonical
form. He does not see the private evaluation guide, historical judge scores,
generator name, condition, target flag, paired twin, revision assignment, or
another response. Canonical rating time stops when the rating rationale is
completed, before feedback prompts. The rating answers are then frozen
in-session; the validated rating and feedback sidecar commit atomically at
submission.

After the full bundle is locked, a `direction-h-blinding-debrief-v1` record
captures whether he recognized an item, remembered prior construction details,
inferred hidden information, encountered an accidental label, or had a timing
interruption. The debrief does not change the original rating. Recognized or
exposed items follow the frozen sensitivity/exclusion rule and remain in study
accounting. `private_map_access_separated` is Charlie's self-report only. The v1
official `review_masking_classification` is always `condition_masked`; it cannot
be upgraded by self-report.

### Phase B2: feedback formation

After each canonical rating response is completed and frozen in-session,
Charlie authors a linked `direction-h-feedback-v1` sidecar without revisiting
those answers. The collector then validates and atomically commits the rating
and sidecar together. The frozen formatter passes only its
identity-free `model_feedback` projection; it does not derive feedback from
numeric scores or add the planted label or an organizer's preferred correction.
The sidecars and their rating hashes freeze before either revision arm is run.

### Phase B3: paired revision

The allocation binds the exact construction-verification, target-brief, and
brief-commitment hashes. The operator runs both `no_feedback` and
`charlie_feedback` for every eligible target under the same
`direction-h-revision-freeze-v1` manifest. Condition order is randomized or
otherwise execution-balanced. Runtime retries follow the same predeclared rule.
Every recorded invocation time must strictly postdate the brief commitment. The
revision model does not receive the condition name, target label, assessment
brief, model blind
map, adjudication rubric answer, or outcomes from the other arm.

In both arms, the model records a truth-blind `revision_diagnosis` with canonical
detected-error flags, findings, and rationale before the revision is assessed.
This diagnosis is a secondary mechanism outcome. It remains in the restricted
revision-output envelope and is not included in the repair-panel bundle; showing
it to adjudicators could reveal the intervention or anchor their judgment.

Before any public repair item is written, the packager applies the frozen
value-level disclosure screen to every complete revised output. Exact arm/field
labels and newly introduced Charlie/feedback-author, reviewer-feedback,
arm-assignment, frozen model/snapshot, or model self-identification phrases
block the entire packaging transaction. The diagnostic records only a signal
code, JSON Pointer, and whether the phrase was already present in visible
source/starting material; it does not copy the surrounding output into a public
log. A match is a protocol deviation. Retain the original envelope and handle
it under the frozen deviation rule—never redact it, request a cosmetic retry,
or substitute another attempt/repeat.

### Phase B4: repair adjudication

A fresh mapping assigns `blinded_revision_id` values to revision outputs. The
panel sees only objects conforming to `direction-h-repair-panel-item-v1`, and
repair-assessment records carry the same blind ID while deliberately carrying no
`feedback_arm` field. Adjudicators do not see feedback source or condition,
Charlie's identity, original generator/model, other adjudications, or aggregate
scores. The display should not put paired conditions side by side unless a
pairwise assessment was separately preregistered; otherwise condition-specific
edits could reveal the intervention.

Independent assessments lock before any reconciliation. The original responses
remain available even when an adjudicated operational label is later created.

The coordinator freezes balanced assignments and exports one self-contained
`RAB-*` directory per assessor. Each export includes only that assessor's
aliased item subset plus a hash-pinned `runtime/` collector/schema package.
Reviewers receive neither the sibling-bundle root nor the private schedule or
arm map, and the collector is confined to an explicit `RAB-*` root. Completed
response bundles are returned through the approved channel and reinserted into
the matching coordinator-staged bundle before the all-assignment lock. Runtime,
item, token/rater-binding, and response hashes must all validate before
unblinding.

### Phase B5: analysis freeze and controlled unblinding

Before opening the condition and target maps, the analyst validates schemas and
hashes, freezes the analysis code/version, primary populations, endpoint mapping,
contrast order, exclusions, and table shells. Unblinding is logged. A discrepancy
after unblinding is corrected only through the declared data-correction process;
the analysis rule is not rewritten to favor the observed pattern.

## 8. Twin separation, order, and contamination

- A reviewer sees no more than one member of a base-packet twin set.
- Pilot and confirmatory base packets do not overlap.
- Human and companion LLM raters may evaluate the same blind item because
  item-level pairing is the purpose, but neither receives the other's response.
- Order is randomized from a recorded seed while balancing failure family and
  controlled status.
- Model outputs are not selected by perceived quality before assignment.
- A known prior exposure, item recognition, browser interruption, discussion
  with another reviewer, or access to a private map is logged at item/session
  level. It is not erased by substituting a better item after response.

Charlie helped develop the study, so expectancy and recognition risk remain even
when labels are hidden. The rater registry and final limitations must preserve
that fact.

## 9. Existing Warrant Study app boundary

The existing feedback collector documents useful controls: separate participant
and organizer access, server-side item delivery, one variant per base packet,
durable per-item saving, timing, and withdrawal. It is still a demo, not an
authorized Direction H confirmatory instrument.

Its current rating record is not identical to `qc-paper-metrics-v1`: it omits
scope calibration, uses one global cannot-judge value, and nulls confidence on
abstention. It also describes a real Dreaddit development bank that is outside
the present synthetic-only authorization. Direction H therefore uses its own
validated artifacts now. A future app adapter or migration must be reviewed,
tested, versioned, and approved before use; a successful UI test does not satisfy
the corpus or rater-study gates.

## 10. Human-review data protections

Even a one-person researcher pilot creates data about a human reviewer. The
study records only task-relevant identity linkage, role, disclosed prior
exposure, ratings, flags, disposition, confidence, rationale, and time. Keep the
identity key separate from item-level exports. Do not infer ability, employment
performance, or authorship merit from the record.

Free-text feedback and rationales can reveal identity or sensitive contextual
information. Review them before any sharing. Public release of Charlie's raw
text requires the applicable consent/authorization; otherwise release coded
features or cleared excerpts. Withdrawal or correction procedures must preserve
the audit trail while respecting the approved deletion requirements.

## 11. Model-processing safeguards

- Synthetic qualification uses only fictional text and does not imply approval
  for the same provider on real text.
- Revision prompts contain only the approved packet, starting output, analytic
  contract, and frozen feedback payload.
- Web, retrieval, memory, and external tools are disabled unless explicitly
  allowed in the frozen synthetic protocol; protected-text use would require
  separate approval.
- Hidden chain-of-thought is neither requested nor stored. The study retains only
  concise source-linked warrants and normal run metadata.
- No discretionary repair of malformed model output is permitted. A format-only
  retry must follow the frozen rule and remain logged.
- Provider/model snapshot, settings, prompt hashes, times, failures, and all
  repeats are retained.

## 12. Deviations, accidental unblinding, and stop conditions

Immediately stop the affected stage and quarantine the item/session when any of
the following occurs:

- protected text is opened or transmitted without a cleared gate;
- a reviewer sees a model, condition, target, twin, or other-rating label;
- a revision model receives a private target label or outside material;
- a repair adjudicator learns the feedback condition or author;
- a schema/identifier/hash mismatch breaks the intended item pairing;
- a private map is included in a reviewer/public bundle;
- an unauthorized person obtains a study secret or protected file; or
- a frozen manifest is changed after outcome inspection.

Record the date, affected records, exposure, containment, and decision. Do not
delete an inconvenient record or rerun only the unfavorable arm. Resume only
after the responsible governance owner determines that the scope remains
authorized and the preregistered deviation rule can be applied. Material changes
create a new version and may require a new institutional determination.

## 13. Release boundary

Subject to the applicable human-data procedure, the synthetic package may
release schemas, prompts, fictional packets, item hashes, frozen manifests,
analysis code, aggregate results, and cleared de-identified case records. Never
release:

- private blind or identity maps;
- participant access or organizer secrets;
- protected real text or reversible short-text hashes without explicit clearance;
- uncleared human rationales or feedback;
- source identifiers omitted from the approved analysis copy; or
- a synthetic result labeled as a real-corpus baseline.

For a protected-data study, the release plan itself must be included in the gate
review before data collection begins.
