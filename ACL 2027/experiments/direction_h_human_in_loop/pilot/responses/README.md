# Locked reviewer responses

`collect_review.py` writes one canonical rating and one linked feedback record
per task into `ratings/` and `feedback/`. No observation belongs here until
Charlie has actually reviewed the full synthetic item.

Do not pre-populate, backfill, or demonstrate the format with invented scores,
rationales, flags, confidence, review times, feedback, or repair outcomes.
Corrections after submission must be appended as explicit amendments under a
future locked protocol; never silently overwrite a substantive first-pass
record.
