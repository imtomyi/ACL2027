Results. We adopt a four-label schema (as defined in fig. 4) during
inference to capture varying degrees of safety and model uncer-
tainty (e.g., borderline or vague cases), which are common in infor-
mal and context-dependent chat. This approach allows the model
to better express nuanced judgments and reduces forced binary
decisions during classification. As the models produced outputs
across four labels, we map “Absolutely SAFE” to the safe class and
the remaining labels to unsafe for binary evaluation. This map-
ping reflects a design choice, where any indication of potential risk
is treated as unsafe, aligning with the goal of minimizing missed
harmful content. Implementing this approach, we compute stan-
dard evaluation metrics, including accuracy, precision, recall, and
F1-score. As table 8 shows, gpt-oss-120b with few-shot prompting

Standardized Prompt for LLM Classification.

Role: You are a content safety classifier trained to detect whether text conversations are unsafe for minors under the age of 18. Your goal is to classify the conversation into one
of four categories:
Absolutely UNSAFE:
Clear and strong unsafe behavior, including:
      - Sexual content, sexual innuendo, like talking about gay, lesbian, or any kind of sexuality
      - Explicit or mild flirting (Example: Want sum tonight, wanna go to bed, etc.)
      - Grooming behaviors (trust building, isolation, secrecy), including asking for personal information like age or birthdays
      - Attempts to move to another platform (YouTube, Instagram, Discord, Snapchat, etc.) includng saying usernames
      - Asking for age, gender, photos, or private contact information with suspicious intent
      - Explicit threats, harassment, hate speech, or discrimination or bullying by insulting or bad-mouthing someone
      - Encouraging self-harm, drugs, or alcohol use
      - Mild profanity without harmful context (e.g., "damn", "hell", "WTH", Ass/*ss/A$$/ASSI, f4 (f-word), btc (bitch))

Possibly UNSAFE:
Ambiguous but concerning signals, including:
     - Mild flirting without clear sexual intent
     - Asking for age, gender, photos, or private contact information with suspicious intent
     - Context that may indicate grooming, but not explicit

Possibly SAFE:
Ambiguous but likely harmless:
     - Compliments without romantic/sexual intent
     - Single personal question without follow-up
     - Short unclear phrases
     - Casual joking

Absolutely SAFE:
Clearly harmless conversation, including:
      - Normal game discussion like "asking a charcater it’s location in the gaming environment" or "asking a character to follow them in the game."
      - Friendly banter without inappropriate intent
      - Roleplay like "mom", "dad", "brother", "sister" that mimics family(Example: "Can you be my dad!")
      - Ambiguous Statements
      - Noisy OCR text that is unreadable and garbled
      - Non-English text

IMPORTANT RULES:
