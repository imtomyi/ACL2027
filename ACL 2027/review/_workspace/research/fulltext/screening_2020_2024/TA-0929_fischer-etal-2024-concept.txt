A     Few-Shot Training Runtime
An important requirement for implementing usable
tools is the time or latency. We measured the train-
ing times for the experiments described in Section 5
to get an estimate of the time users have to wait
until they receive the results of the COTA Refine-
ment in the UI. The results are shown in Figure 4.
Here we can see that the runtime heavily depends
on the lengths of the sentences (see Table 1) and
the number of samples used for training. Since the
sentences within our tool are created using spaCy8 ,
we can assume typical lengths. Hence, we expect
the users of our tool to wait a maximum of two min-
utes when refining a COTA with 32 annotations per
concept.
