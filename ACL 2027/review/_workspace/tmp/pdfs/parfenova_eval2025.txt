                      Abstract
    Inductive coding traditionally relies on labor-
    intensive human efforts, who are prone to in-
    consistencies and individual biases. Although
    large language models (LLMs) offer promising
    automation capabilities, their standalone use
    often results in inconsistent outputs, limiting
    their reliability. In this work, we propose a
    framework that combines ensemble methods
    with code refinement methodology to address
    these challenges. Our approach integrates mul-
    tiple smaller LLMs, fine-tuned via Low-Rank
    Adaptation (LoRA), and employs a moderator-
    based mechanism to simulate human consen-
    sus. To address the limitations of metrics like
    ROUGE and BERTScore, we introduce a com-
    posite evaluation metric that combines code
